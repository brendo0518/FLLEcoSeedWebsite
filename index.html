What is EcoSeed?

A student concept for reaching places that are hard to reach on foot.

EcoSeed is a proposed electric-powered drone system. A real drone carries a custom 3D-printed seed dispenser so that locally appropriate native seeds could be placed in approved restoration areas that are difficult to access.

Over the longer term we imagine habitat mapping, monitoring after seeding, and a service that councils, governments and licensed conservation organisations could use. Those are ideas we are researching. They are not services we offer.

Where EcoSeed is right now

  • Prototype  Drone with a 3D-printed seed dispenser, being built and tested.
  • Concept  Habitat mapping and monitoring after seeding.
  • Proposed  Purchase, rental or managed-service models for approved users.

EcoSeed is not an established company and its environmental benefits are not yet proven.

The biodiversity challenge

Habitats are being lost, split up and worn down.

Restoration is slow, careful work, and some of the places that most need it are the hardest to get to.

Habitat loss

When native vegetation is cleared, the plants and animals that depend on it lose food, shelter and breeding places.

Fragmentation

Remaining bushland can end up as isolated patches, which makes it harder for wildlife and pollinators to move between them.

Hard-to-reach sites

Steep, rugged or remote ground can make conventional planting slow, costly or unsafe for people.
