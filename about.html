Meet the Team Behind EcoSeed

A student robotics team in Western Australia, working on a real environmental question.

Team introduction

Ten students, one Innovation Project.

EcoSeed is being developed by , a 10-member student robotics team from taking part in FIRST LEGO League. Five members work mainly on the Innovation Project, while the whole team shares ideas, testing and feedback.

We are students learning as we go. We ask experts for advice, test our ideas, and are open about what is still unproven.

Why we chose biodiversity

BIOGLOW asks teams to look at living systems. We chose biodiversity because native habitats in Western Australia are special, and we wanted to explore whether technology could help look after them.

Our mission

To explore, test and honestly document whether a drone seed dispenser could help restore native habitats in approved, hard-to-reach places.

Our vision

A future where restoration crews have more careful, well-advised tools, and where students are part of the conversation about caring for nature.
