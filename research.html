Innovation With a Purpose

What we hope EcoSeed could do for nature and communities, and how we are checking our thinking. These are goals, not proven outcomes.

Goals

What we are working toward.

Biodiversity and habitat restoration

Explore whether targeted native seeding could help degraded sites begin to recover, in places where approved restoration is needed.

Native plants and wildlife

If seeding succeeds, more native plant cover could provide food and shelter for native animals and pollinators over time.

Conservation workers and communities

Could help crews avoid some difficult or risky terrain, and give local people a way to see and support restoration work.

All of the above are potential benefits. We have not measured any of them.
